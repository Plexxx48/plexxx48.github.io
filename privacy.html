Plexxx48 – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

1. Overview

Plexxx48 is a personal tool used by its owner to publish videos on their own TikTok account. This policy explains what data the app handles.

2. Data we access

When the owner connects their TikTok account, the app receives an access token and a refresh token through TikTok Login, with the permissions the owner approves (basic profile information and permission to publish videos). The app also reads the account's posting options and the publishing status of videos it sends.

3. How data is used

This data is used only to publish videos to the connected TikTok account and to check the status of those posts.

4. Storage and security

Tokens are stored locally on the owner's own device or server and are not shared with, sold to, or disclosed to third parties.

5. Third-party services

To produce videos, the app uses AI services for text, image and voice generation. These services receive only the video script and image descriptions, never TikTok account data or tokens.

6. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until the owner disconnects the account. The owner can revoke access at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, and can delete the stored tokens file to remove all local data.

7. Children

The app is not intended for children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version is always available on this page.
